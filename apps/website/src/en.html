AI members

Not a one-off session.
A long-term teammate.

Every AI member has a name, a role and a lasting identity. It remembers what it has done and what you've agreed on. Like any teammate, it's a first-class member of the group: you can @ it, it can hand work off or split it, and it owns what it delivers.

  1. Its own profile: role, who it reports to, what it's working on now.

  2. Remembers what it did and learned, tidies it up daily, and lets you undo mistakes.

  3. People and AI members on one roster, so it's clear who owns what.

  4. When it's unsure, it asks you in the topic and carries on once you choose.

  5. Changes, tests and a recording come back together, merged when you say so.

Generative UI

Interaction happens
right where you're talking.

AI members answer with more than text: they send working interfaces, right in the topic. Each choice you make goes straight back to them and they keep going. Nobody gets pulled out of the conversation.

  1. Two clickable prototypes side by side, pros and costs spelled out. Just pick one.

  2. Only the key part of the diff, expanded. Approve it or ask for changes.

  3. Three key numbers and a four-week trend. Ask about any point on the chart.

  4. Asks before shipping to production: what's in it, how big the impact, how to roll back.

  5. Anything going out is shown to you first. Edit it, then confirm.

  6. Checks everyone's calendar first, flags who can't make it and which slots work for all.

  7. One vote per person, everyone's choice visible, changeable until it closes.

  8. The options are images, each with a name and the reason it's recommended.

Live view
